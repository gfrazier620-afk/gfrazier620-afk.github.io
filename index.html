About Me

            

            

                

                    

                        I’m transitioning into cybersecurity after a 20-year career as a golf professional—a role that built
                        my discipline, focus, and ability to perform under pressure.
                    

                    

                        Golf taught me how to analyze performance, adapt quickly, and continuously improve. I now apply that
                        same mindset to security: understanding systems, identifying weaknesses, and strengthening defenses.
                    

                    

                        I’m actively building hands-on experience in networking, Linux, security fundamentals, and SOC workflows,
                        with a strong interest in blue team operations, threat detection, and incident response.
                    

                    

                        My goal is to contribute to a security-focused team where I can grow as an analyst, support defensive
                        operations, and help protect critical systems and data.

About Me

I’m currently transitioning into the field of cybersecurity after spending 20 years as a golf professional—a career that shaped my work ethic, discipline, and ability to perform under pressure.

Golf taught me how to stay focused, adapt quickly, and continuously improve. Whether it was coaching players, analyzing performance, or managing high-pressure situations, I developed a mindset built on problem-solving and resilience—skills I now bring into cybersecurity.

Today, I’m actively training and building hands-on experience in areas like networking, Linux, and security fundamentals. I’m especially interested in entry-level roles where I can contribute, learn, and grow—such as a SOC Analyst or IT support role with a security focus.

This transition is driven by more than just career growth—it’s about building a stable and meaningful future for my family, while pursuing a field that challenges me every day.

I’m excited to connect with others in the cybersecurity space, learn from experienced professionals, and continue developing the skills needed to protect systems and data in an ever-evolving landscape.
